# Version 1.0.0

Initial public reproducibility release accompanying the manuscript.

- Final three-seed protocol for stochastic detectors (42–44).
- Labelled validation-F1 and normal-only q99/q99.5 threshold policies.
- Point-wise, event-level, delay, false-alarm, and moving-block metrics.
- Gas-Pipeline attack-label-budget and split-sensitivity experiments.
- Full HAI normal training sequence, with the four-event limitation disclosed.
- Per-seed scalar results and representative seed-42 raw score arrays.

Raw third-party datasets are excluded and must be obtained from their original
sources.
